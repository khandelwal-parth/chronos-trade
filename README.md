# ⏱ Chronos — Time Travel Trading

> **Go back. Trade smarter. Learn.**

Chronos is a historical stock trading sandbox that lets you step into the past and trade at real market prices — hour by hour. Pick any stock, pick any date from the last two years, and experience how the market actually unfolded. No real money, no risk, pure learning.

🌐 **Live App:** [chronos-trade.onrender.com](https://chronos-trade.onrender.com/)

---

## What Is Chronos?

Most trading simulators use fake, random price data. Chronos uses **real historical market prices** to recreate what it was actually like to trade on any given day. You'll feel the uncertainty of not knowing what comes next — because the chart reveals itself hour by hour, just like it did in real time.

It's the closest thing to a time machine for traders.

---

## Features

- **Real Historical Data** — Trade at actual prices from the last 2 years of market history
- **Hour-by-Hour Chart Reveal** — Prices unfold progressively so you experience the market as it happened
- **Buy & Sell at Will** — Place trades at any point during the session using real bid/ask prices
- **Starting Cash Control** — Set your own starting bankroll and track your P&L
- **Historic Market Moments** — One-click presets to relive famous market events (crashes, rallies, earnings blowouts)
- **Trade History Log** — Full record of every buy and sell you make during a session
- **Any Stock Symbol** — Enter any ticker and jump to any date

---

## How to Use

1. **Enter a stock symbol** (e.g. `AAPL`, `TSLA`, `SPY`)
2. **Pick a date** from the last 2 years
3. **Set your starting cash**
4. **Click "Start Trading"** — the chart begins to unfold
5. **Buy and sell** as price action develops, just like you would in a real session
6. **Review your trade history** and see how your decisions played out

Or — click one of the **Historic Market Moments** to instantly jump to a famous trading event and see if you could have caught the move.
